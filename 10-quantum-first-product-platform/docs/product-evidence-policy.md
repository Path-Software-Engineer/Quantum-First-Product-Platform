# Product and evidence policy v1

1. Classify every statement as verified fact, sourced inference, explicit
   hypothesis or synthetic test content. Never promote one category by styling.
2. A public claim needs a named owner, source/reference identifier, observed
   date or source version, stated limitation, review decision and reviewer
   distinct from its owner. A link alone is not approval.
3. `pending_human_review` AI bundles are rejected for publication. An import
   must preserve producer project/commit, schema version, hash, generated time,
   method version and evidence index. The software stores no AI runtime state.
4. Pricing, time-to-value and licensing are scenarios, not quotations,
   contracts or measured performance. Do not fabricate buyer interviews,
   customer logos, revenue or risk reduction percentages.
5. Claims about future quantum capability require a current classical or
   post-quantum path, a maturity condition and a kill gate. No implied quantum
   advantage or compliance certification.
6. Approved/public artifacts are immutable. Corrections create a new version;
   previous versions retain provenance and audit history.

The trust center must show these categories and explain what the software has
actually verified versus what still awaits human or market validation.
