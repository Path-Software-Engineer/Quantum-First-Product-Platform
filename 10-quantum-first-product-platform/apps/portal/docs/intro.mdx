# Platform contract

This is the Sprint 1 development portal, not a deployed or released product.
The source of truth for product state is the NestJS API and PostgreSQL; this
Docusaurus site renders a public one-pager only from an accepted, versioned
build identifier.

## The first vertical slice

An authorized workspace member creates a draft product version, links
capabilities and use cases, submits claims with provenance, obtains a separate
review decision, approves and publishes the version, then generates an
immutable public one-pager with a source SHA-256. Cross-workspace
access, self-approval and unsourced claims must fail.

See the [trust and evidence boundary](/trust) before interpreting any future
business or post-quantum statement. The AI Project 55 handoff is still pending
human review and is not represented here as approved content.
